# Homepage and site-wide redesign

Scope: the homepage first, then every route in the same world (`/blog`, `/data`, `/methods`, `/journey`, `/gallery`, `/privacy`). Visitor mode: Persuade on the homepage, Read on articles.

Audience and job: a recruiter or hiring manager for Scientist / Scientist II roles in ag-bio R&D, checking whether the person can do the work on the resume. Success is that they open and read one piece of work.

Constraints: all text and content stay word for word. Colours, fonts, icons and dark mode are free to change. The owner asked for "warm and industry professional" and "soft and tactile" controls, and said a startup-template look would feel wrong. The first screen must show the person and the role first.

Resolved: one theme, no dark mode. Reading pages open on a green header room and continue on rose plaster.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is hung as a solo exhibition about one scientist. The person is the opening wall; each project is an exhibit in the rooms that follow. It refuses the white page with a centred intro over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Each section is a room painted in one flat coat: merlot `#6b1d35` for the opening wall, vine green `#1f4d3a` for the work, with paper-white `#fbf6ee` wall lettering, label-card stock `#f6f1e6`, ink `#1d1418` and a brass `#e9c46a` for role lines. No gradients, washes or glass. Display type is Bricolage Grotesque, set very large like vinyl wall lettering; text is Public Sans. Exhibits sit on label cards of one fixed format. Buttons stand proud of the wall on a hard lower edge and press in when clicked.

STORY: The visitor meets the person and the role, reads the one-line statement, sees real exhibits one room down, and opens one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed merlot wall. Navigation as a line of wall signage across the top. Name at left in white display type about 124px on two lines, role in brass under it, the statement at about 38px and the open-to line under that, then two buttons with "Explore research" as the primary. Framed portrait hung at right, about 400 by 480 with a mat and a dark frame edge, and a small label card beside it. The top of the green room shows across the bottom with the first three project labels.

FORM: A solo exhibition (painted walls, wall lettering, framed work, object labels), candidate 7 of 7 on the second hand. seed:ebfd98f4227eb7f6. Raises carried in: one flat coat per room; one index sign that reaches every room; one fixed label format; wall colour used at room scale, never as a small accent. Signature interaction: buttons and exhibit labels press in on a hard edge; moving from one room to the next changes the wall colour once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
